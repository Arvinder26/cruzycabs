## Changes released

The audit fixes, current SEO/contact/thank-you/review content, responsive improvements and consent-controlled Google Analytics configuration are now deployed. See `AUDIT-FIXES.md` for the per-finding scope and remaining business/provider gates.

The Google tag is **G-1RCJ5G2SKB**. It loads only after an explicit analytics choice, measures allowlisted public pages, and excludes booking/account/staff URLs and private query/fragment data. Decline and withdrawal remain supported.

During live acceptance, the initial analytics request returned 204 because the browser's consent timestamp was slightly ahead of the hosted server clock. This also matched the reported failed `analytics-frame` load. The correction permits at most five minutes of skew for the **explicit consent cookie only**, retains the 180-day expiry and rejects materially future-dated choices. Authentication, booking/quote expiry and access-control logic were not relaxed. The analytics document explicitly declares inline HTML; Vercel strips Content-Type on empty 204 responses but retains Content-Disposition: inline.

A new browser regression deliberately advances the browser clock by two seconds and verifies the first accepted page view, 200 HTML frame responses, zero download events and no CSP/page errors. Both accepted public-page frames returned `text/html; charset=utf-8` and `Content-Disposition: inline` on the final live build.
